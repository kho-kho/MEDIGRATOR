Medical Waste Segregation Directory

Standardized color-coded disposal containers and treatment guidelines according to WHO and CPCB rules.

🟡 Yellow Bin Infectious / Soiled

Anatomical, Soiled & Fabric Waste

Dressings, swabs, gauze soaked with blood or body fluids, soiled plaster casts, infectious masks, and surgical gowns.

Treatment: High-temperature Incineration (>1050°C) or Plasma Pyrolysis.
🔴 Red Bin Recyclable Plastic

Contaminated Recyclable Plastics

Examination gloves, IV bottles, catheters, plastic tubing, plastic syringe barrels (without needles), urine bags.

Treatment: Autoclaving / Microwaving followed by mechanical shredding and plastic recycling.
⚪ White Box Sharps (Puncture-Proof)

Metallic & Puncture Sharps

Hypodermic injection needles, syringes with fixed needles, scalpels, surgical blades, lumbar puncture needles.

Treatment: Autoclaving / Dry Heat Sterilization followed by sharp-pit encapsulation.
🔵 Blue Bin / Box Glassware

Medicine Vials & Glass Articles

Glass ampoules, vacutainers, broken glass bottles, contaminated glass slides, metallic orthopedic implants.

Treatment: Chemical disinfection (1-2% Sodium Hypochlorite) or autoclaving followed by glass recycling.
⚫ Black / Green General Municipal

Non-Hazardous Solid Waste

Office paper, packaging wrappers, non-infected sterile item packaging, cafeteria food waste.

Treatment: Standard municipal waste recycling or sanitary landfill.
